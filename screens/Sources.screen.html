How this dashboard is compiled

  • Figures come from company results releases, regulatory filings and exchange announcements where one was found. Otherwise they come from news coverage, call records or data providers, and are labelled as such.
  • Every figure carries a source. A figure that was not found is shown as n/f. Nothing is estimated.
  • Outlook statements and targets are the companies' own claims and carry the company_claim label.
  • Growth rates, margins, segment totals and period sums are recomputed by tools/check.py and must agree with the stated figures.
  • News summaries are written in plain words from the sources listed. They are not quotations.
  • The dashboard holds public information only. It is not investment advice.
